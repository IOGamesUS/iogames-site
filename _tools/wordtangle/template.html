How to play
  • Four words make the sides of the rectangle. Words share their corner letters.
  • Arrows show which way each word reads. On harder days the arrows are hidden: a hint can show them.
  • Tap a square or a clue, then type. Tap a corner again to switch words.
  • Fill every square, then Check. Correct words lock in. You get 3 checks.
  • Your score is checks used plus hints used, like golf: beat the par. Ties go to the faster time.
