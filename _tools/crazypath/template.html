How to play
  • Draw one path that visits every card exactly once.
  • Step up, down, left or right, never diagonally. Each card must match the one before it by suit or by rank, like Crazy Eights.
  • Start on any card. Tap cards one at a time, or drag your finger across them.
  • Tap a card already on your path to back up to it, or tap Start over. Each back-up costs a stroke, and so does each hint. Changing your first card before you move is free: tap it again, or tap a different card.
  • Your score is back-ups plus hints, like golf: beat the par.
