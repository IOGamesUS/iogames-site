How to play
  • Place the cards from your hand so every row and every column makes the poker hand written beside it.
  • Hands: straight flush, four of a kind, three of a kind, straight, flush, two pair, pair, high card (none of those). A line counts as its best hand only.
  • Aces are high or low in a straight (A 2 3 or Q K A), but straights don't wrap (no K A 2).
  • Tap a card in your hand, then an empty square. Tap a placed card to take it back. Darker cards are fixed in place.
  • Fill every square, then Check. You get 3 checks. Your score is checks plus hints, like golf: beat the par.
