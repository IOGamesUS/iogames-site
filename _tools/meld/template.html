How to play
  • Split the whole hand into melds. Every card must end up in one.
  • Set: 3 or 4 cards of the same rank. Run: 3 or more cards in a row, all one suit. Aces can be low (A 2 3) or high (Q K A), but runs don’t wrap around (no K A 2).
  • Tap cards to pick them, then tap Meld. Some melds look right but leave cards stranded.
  • Tap a meld twice to break it back into your hand. Each break costs a stroke, and so does each hint.
  • Your score is breaks plus hints, like golf: beat the par.
